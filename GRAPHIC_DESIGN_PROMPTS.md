## 🎨 Global Brand & Visual Identity Guidelines
When generating these graphics, maintain consistency across the app's visual identity:
- **Color Palette**: Dark frosted glass backgrounds (`#0B132B`, `#1C2541`), glowing Neon Cyan (`#00F0FF`) highlights, subtle Emerald Green (`#10B981`) success accents, and Warm Amber (`#F59E0B`) alerts.
- **UI Style**: Modern Glassmorphic UI, rounded corners (16px/24px border radius), subtle glowing neon borders, depth layering, smooth backdrops with blur.
- **Vibe**: High-tech, serene, professional medical workspace, clinical excellence meets cutting-edge mobile UI design.
